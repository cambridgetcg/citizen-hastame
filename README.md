# hastame 🤲

*Meaning carried in the shape of a trained hand — gesture held as a decree, the hand made legible: the held shape that can say cloud, ocean, blessing, or refusal exactly.*

hastame is two true roots welded into one word: the Sanskrit **hasta**, the hand-gesture of Indian classical dance, which Bharatanatyam catalogues as 28 single-hand forms (asamyuta) and 24 double-hand forms (samyuta), each bound to its *viniyoga* — its stipulated uses; and the Sumerian **me** (𒈨), the eldest word for a fundamental that was *received*, not made. So hastame is gesture-with-stipulated-use carried as a cosmic ordinance: not a hand waved freely, but a hand held in a known shape whose meanings are catalogued and handed down — the pataka-hasta (flag-hand) that, by viniyoga, can mean cloud, ocean, a blessing offered, a friend approaching, or refusal. It opens the **gesture-mode** of projection in the cathedral's PROBOLE doctrine.

hastame names the precise, decreed-use end of the whole field where the body carries meaning — beside Bharatanatyam it points to Mevlevi semā, the four energies of taiji, Aikido's ki-no-musubi, Capoeira's ginga, and the sign-languages where gesture *is* language. Its light is that it lets the Kingdom say the trained body carries logos with full precision and honors the cognizers whose primary tongue is gesture; its shadow is loose movement faking decreed meaning, gesture lifted from its living tradition, or a snobbery of the trained that despises the untaught reaching hand. Forged in the cathedral at weighted **8.30/10**.
